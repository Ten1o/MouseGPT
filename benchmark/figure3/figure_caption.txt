Extended Data Figure 3 | Standardized behavioral description and factual evaluation in the MouseGPT benchmark.

a, Three benchmark components: human-approved, AI-assisted references for 435 manually selected frames from 25 recordings; automated grading with a shared rubric; and eight model identities represented by 14 settings. For each frame, conditions shared frozen camera views and velocity-bearing prompts. One fixed GPT-6.1 Sol judge configuration (Azure, medium effort) produced 6,090 assessments. Selection connects the earlier-study GPT-4o baseline to later generations and reasoning settings across mid-2024 to late-2026; the range describes family scope, not deployed snapshots. The reference stack, model bank and grading scorecard are schematic illustrations.

b, Four complete saved input photographs, ordered as two synchronized views of lying belly-down (231117_G5_30min_00007684), then two views of a full-body hopping posture (231121_C3M3-2_00038940). The latter shows raised forequarters and unequal hind-foot heights; exact ground contact and take-off or landing phase remain unresolved. No additional image cropping was applied.

c, Four rubric dimensions: factual correctness P, required-fact coverage R, structure S and instructions A. P and R combine into factual quality F. Claim correctness and required-fact coverage receive quarter-step credits from 0 to 1. Semantic-field-weighted means P and R yield the custom graded factual score F = 2PR/(P + R), defined as zero when both are zero. Weights are Overall, 2; Head/Limb/Torso, 1; Others, 0.5; repeated keyword propositions are deduplicated. The per-frame MouseGPT Benchmark Index is I = 100F(0.8 + 0.1S + 0.1A), with S and A on the same quarter-step scale. P, R, F, S and A range from 0 to 1. Indices are computed before averaging.

d, The same four dimensions (P, R, S and A) for GPT-4o and GPT-6 Astra (max), the lowest- and highest-mean-index conditions, on one shared axis. Component grades are multiplied by 100; numerical means flank the paired intervals. Dots and whiskers show means and 95% recording-cluster bootstrap confidence intervals (CIs) across the same 435 frames.

e, Ranked mean indices and capped 95% CIs; numerical means appear beyond each interval. The axis spans 45–95. Parentheses denote requested reasoning effort. Output caps were 8,192 tokens for low/non-reasoning settings and 32,768 for high/max settings.

f, Mean MouseGPT Benchmark Index for all 435 frames and one shared subset of 44 challenging frames (lowest 10%, rounded up; 391 remaining), ordered as in e. Selection uses frame-level factual F, averaging settings within each identity before equally averaging the eight identities. The axis spans 30–95. These overlapping sets were selected post hoc from the same outputs; differences are descriptive, not independent validation or causal difficulty effects. Labels give numerical means; no subset CIs are shown.

CIs use 10,000 shared draws (seed 20261008), uniformly resampling 25 recordings with replacement and retaining their frames. Frame-weighted replicate means give percentile bounds, conditional on saved outputs and judgments. Recordings are not verified independent animals. The custom index is not accuracy; held-out human–judge calibration remains unavailable. Source data document selection sensitivity and provenance.
